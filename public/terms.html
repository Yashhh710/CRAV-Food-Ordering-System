Terms & Conditions

1. Academic Platform & Non-Commercial Nature

CRAV – From Craving to Doorstep is an academic college project developed for educational, curriculum evaluation, and software demonstration purposes. CRAV is not a commercial fast-food entity, is not affiliated with KFC or Yum! Brands, and does not sell or distribute physical food items for actual delivery.

Disclaimer of Commercial Activity: By using this website, you understand that all product listings, orders, payments, coupons, and table reservations represent simulation data only.

2. Account Registration & Security

When you create an account on CRAV:

  • You agree to provide valid information (First Name, Last Name, and Email Address).
  • You are responsible for maintaining the confidentiality of your login credentials.
  • Accounts require 6-digit email OTP verification before activation.
  • We reserve the right to prune or reset demonstration accounts periodically to maintain database hygiene.

3. Menu Items, Pricing & Descriptions

All dishes, ingredients, combo offerings, nutritional facts, and prices shown on the menu originate from static demonstration JSON datasets (`assets/js/crav_menu.json`). Prices in Indian Rupees (₹) are purely illustrative to demonstrate cart calculations, coupons, and discounts.

4. Ordering & Table Reservations

Placing an order or requesting a table reservation exercises the backend REST endpoints:

  • Orders are stored in the project database with stages progressing from Order Confirmed to Delivered.
  • Table reservations allocate simulated tables (Table T-01 through T-15) based on real backend collision detection to ensure no double bookings for the same time slot.
  • Digital tickets and verification QR codes are produced solely for software testing and verification demonstration.

5. Demo QR Payment Simulation

The integrated QR payment flow is a simulated payment gateway:

  • Scanning the QR code using Google Lens or a smartphone scanner opens the mobile demo payment view (`pay-mobile.html`).
  • Clicking "Pay Now" simulates short network processing and marks the order as paid on the server.
  • No real financial transaction occurs. No bank accounts, UPI IDs, credit cards, or payment gateways (such as Razorpay or Stripe) are connected.

6. Order Tracking & Delivery Partner Simulation

The live order tracking page (`order-tracking.html`) displays realistic stage progressions for UX demonstration. Delivery partners, vehicles, and status timings are generated from our simulation database (`assets/js/deliveryboy.json`). No actual physical couriers are dispatched.

7. Cancellation & Refund Policy

Because no real monetary exchange occurs on this platform, all transactions carry zero financial value. No actual refunds, credits, or chargebacks are applicable or possible.

8. Intellectual Property & Fair Use

Product imagery, icons, and branding elements are utilized under fair use guidelines for educational, student portfolio, and college evaluation purposes. All registered trademarks and logos belong to their respective owners.

9. Limitation of Liability

The CRAV platform is provided on an "as-is" and "as-available" basis without warranties of any kind. The creators, university, and project contributors shall not be liable for any perceived reliance on food delivery or reservation fulfillment.

10. Inquiries & Academic Inquiries

If you have any feedback or academic review inquiries regarding these Terms and Conditions, please contact us through our Contact Page or by emailing hello@crav.example.
