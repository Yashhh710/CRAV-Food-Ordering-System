Privacy Policy

1. Academic Project & Demonstration Context

CRAV – From Craving to Doorstep is an educational software engineering project created solely for demonstration, testing, and portfolio presentation. It is not a commercial enterprise, restaurant franchise, or affiliate of any multinational food brand. All features, orders, and payment mechanisms are realistic simulations.

Important Note: This platform is designed to emulate production-grade food ordering. No real monetary transactions, live food shipments, or commercial contracts are conducted.

2. Information We Collect

We only collect information directly submitted by you during account creation, checkout, or table reservation:

  • Identity Information: First Name and Last Name provided during registration.
  • Account Credentials: Email address and securely hashed password.
  • Delivery Addresses: Street address, apartment/suite, state/city, and PIN code saved for order fulfillment.
  • Order & Reservation Records: Selected items, subtotal, discount, delivery time preferences, and table party size.

Note: Mobile phone numbers are not mandatory and are not stored or required to browse, register, or place demo orders.

3. Account Creation & JWT Authentication

User sessions are managed using industry-standard JSON Web Tokens (JWT). When you log in, our backend issues an encrypted access token stored within your browser's local storage. This token securely authenticates subsequent actions such as viewing previous orders, managing saved addresses, and reserving tables without transmitting your plain-text password repeatedly.

4. Email OTP Verification

To verify the authenticity of registered email addresses, our backend generates a secure 6-digit One-Time Password (OTP) with an expiration window of 5 minutes. Real emails are dispatched using Gmail SMTP when server credentials are provided. In local demonstration environments without SMTP credentials, OTP codes are logged directly to the server terminal to allow seamless evaluation.

5. Password Hashing & Storage Security

Your security is paramount. Passwords are never stored in plain text. CRAV utilizes salted bcrypt cryptographic hashing (or PBKDF2 with SHA-512) before any credential is saved to our database. This prevents reversible exposure of passwords even in the event of database inspection.

6. Database Storage Architecture

All application entities (users, verified accounts, delivery addresses, demo orders, and table bookings) are persisted using a persistent database engine supporting MongoDB connections or local JSON storage (`data/db.json`). All data belongs strictly to the authenticated user ID and cannot be accessed across different accounts.

7. Demo QR Payment Simulation

CRAV features a realistic, simulated QR payment workflow. Scanning the QR code with Google Lens or a mobile scanner directs you to an internal demonstration payment page (`pay-mobile.html`). Clicking "Pay Now" generates a simulated transaction identifier (`TXN-DEMO-...`). No real banking credentials, debit/credit cards, UPI PINs, or money transfers are ever requested, processed, or stored.

8. Browser Local Storage & Session Data

We do not track you across third-party websites or deploy tracking advertising cookies. We utilize standard browser localStorage solely for functional features:

  • crav_cart: Retaining your selected menu items.
  • crav_auth_token: Maintaining your active authenticated session.
  • crav_wishlist: Preserving your bookmarked favorite dishes.

9. Your Data Rights & Deletion Requests

You have full ownership of your data on CRAV:

  • You may view, update, or remove your saved addresses at any time via the User Dashboard.
  • You may change your account password through the dashboard security tab.
  • To request complete removal of your demo account and all associated order history, you can contact our project team or clear your browser session data.

10. Contact & Project Support

For questions regarding this privacy document, technical evaluation, or academic assessment of the CRAV architecture, please reach out through our Contact Page or email our student project lead at hello@crav.example.
